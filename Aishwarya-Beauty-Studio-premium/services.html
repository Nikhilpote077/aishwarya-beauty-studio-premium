FAQ

Common Questions

Do you offer a trial session before the wedding day?

Yes — every bridal package includes one complimentary trial session so we can finalise your exact look together before the big day.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Absolutely. Aishwarya is available for destination bookings across India; travel and stay charges are quoted separately based on location.

What products do you use?

Only premium, dermatologically tested and skin-friendly brands — all tools are sanitised and single-use applicators are used for every client.

How far in advance should I book?

For wedding dates, we recommend booking 2–3 months in advance, especially during peak wedding season (Nov–Feb).
