Her Story

A Passion Turned Into A Craft

Aishwarya Pote began her journey in bridal makeup artistry over five years ago, driven by a simple belief: every bride deserves to feel like the most beautiful version of herself on her big day — not a version of someone else.

Since then, she has trained under renowned names in the industry, built a loyal community of brides across Karnataka and beyond, and developed a signature style that blends HD precision with soft, natural elegance.
